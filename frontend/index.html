Drop any PDF, JPG, or PNG of your blood test, pathology report, or prescription. No manual typing required.

Clinical OCR Extraction

MedSaathi extracts numerical values, preserves data types, auto-corrects unit errors (e.g. mgldl to mg/dL), and compares 4 reference boundaries.

Our Purpose

About MedSaathi & Our Vision

Empowering every citizen with health literacy in the language of their heart.

🎯

Our Mission

To eliminate the medical language barrier across India and ensure that no patient is left in the dark or frightened by diagnostic numbers. We believe healthcare transparency is a fundamental right, and understanding your lab results should be as easy and comforting as speaking with an empathetic family doctor.

  • ✓ Democratizing health literacy without paywalls
  • ✓ Bridging the gap between pathology labs and patients
  • ✓ Supporting 10+ native Indian languages authentically
💡

Why We Built This

Over 70% of medical diagnostic reports in India are delivered exclusively in complex English terminology with abbreviations like eGFR, SGOT, MCV, HbA1c. For millions of hardworking families and elderly citizens, reading these numbers causes extreme anxiety or dangerous delays in seeking timely care.

We created MedSaathi to give every person instant, compassionate clarity in their mother tongue—complete with healthy dietary guidance and a ready checklist of questions for their next doctor visit.
